Where I Focus
My Work

⬡
Area 01

Spatial Keynesian Economics

Developing a spatial Keynesian framework examining how fiscal policy effects and demand spillovers propagate across regions. Using European NUTS2 data to explore public sector shocks and coordination failures that deepen contractions.

Spatial Econometrics Fiscal Policy Panel Data
◈
Area 02

Structural Transformation & Development

Examining how economies' industrial composition evolves and what role policy plays in steering this change — bridging theoretical frameworks with empirical analysis of production linkages and institutional capacity.

Input-Output Network Analysis Industrial Policy
◎
Area 03

Green Transition Macroeconomics

Demand-driven models of energy capacity investment exploring how economies navigate the energy transition while maintaining employment and growth, with attention to distributional implications of climate policy.

Climate Policy Demand Models Simulation
